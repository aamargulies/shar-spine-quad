How did Skylake do in comparison with Graviton and EMR?

At n=4096, tuned+spine (the shipped path on each host):

                      warm ns   cold ns   SKX ratio warm   SKX ratio cold
  SKX  (Xeon 8175M)      27.5     131.1       1.00x            1.00x
  EMR  (Xeon 8559C)      17.9      46.2       0.65x            0.35x
  GV4  (Neoverse V2)     16.2      36.4       0.59x            0.28x

SKX is slowest across the board: ~1.5x EMR and ~1.7x GV4 warm, ~2.8x EMR
and ~3.6x GV4 cold at n=4096. The cold gap is much larger than the warm
gap, which is exactly what you'd expect -- SKX (a) runs the scalar
interpolation loop downclocked under the AVX-512 license (even though the
zmm path is gated off, the surrounding AVX-512 state still taxes it),
(b) sits on a shared-tenant EC2 LLC with noisier neighbors, and (c) has a
narrower OoO window to hide DRAM round-trips. EMR and GV4 are in roughly
the same ballpark as each other (EMR slightly slower warm, slightly slower
cold -- but within the noise floor of a single EC2 session).

Relative wins from the optimizations track cleanly across hosts, with one
SKX exception:

                 compile-time n=4096 unroll       two-level spine D vs A
                 (warm / cold delta vs general-n) (warm / cold delta)
  SKX            -1.5%  / noise                   -41%  / -74%
  EMR            -22%   / -30%                    -40%  / -71%
  GV4            -10%   / noise                   (not yet ported)

The two-level spine structure is basically uarch-agnostic on x86: SKX's
A->D deltas (-41% warm, -74% cold) are within a hair of EMR's (-40%,
-71%). SKX's absolute numbers are much worse (237 -> 139 ns vs EMR 142
-> 85 ns warm), but the fraction of the baseline you get back is the
same. That's the real headline from this session -- the optimization is
buying back an architecture-agnostic structural cost, not a SKX-specific
mitigation.

The compile-time-n=4096 unroll is the lone exception: ~0 on SKX, ~-10%
on GV4, ~-22% on EMR. Prior prediction was "biggest win on SKX" (narrow
OoO + downclock -> branch control should hurt more). Wrong -- SKX cold
is dominated by memory, not branch overhead, so removing interpolation-
loop branches has nothing to buy back.
