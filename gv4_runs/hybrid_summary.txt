Hybrid spine_{256,1024,4096}h = straight-line quaternary descent
(like _spine_N unroll) + branchless 2-probe finish at n=2 (like _pad
but with no sentinels because num_blocks is known at compile time):

    lo = base + (spine[base] < pos) + (spine[base+1] < pos);

This captures both sawtooth mechanisms at once.

Hybrid is the winner at every high-foot size in both regimes, against
every existing variant. Ship recommendation: ship hybrid at
n ∈ {256, 1024, 4096} for narrow-API callers; retire _spine_4096 (the
prior unroll) since hybrid strictly dominates it.
